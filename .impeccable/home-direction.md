# Home room direction

Scope: replace the main 3D preview with a single illustrated shared living room and the approved pixel characters. Experience mode; no customization, social features, furnishing system, or character interactions in this step.

## Direction contract
THESIS: a quiet fixed-view home, inspired by domestic life games, with two personal pixel characters inhabiting an original soft illustrated room.
OWN-WORLD: dusty rose, sage, cream and honey wood by day; plum ambient light and warm lamps by night. Nunito remains the brand voice. Rounded, legible controls stay outside the art.
STORY: open the app, see the two approved characters at home, notice the real local time reflected in the window and lamp.
FIRST VIEWPORT: compact Nestle header, short home title, one dominant 4:3 room with an unobtrusive local clock. Below it, a short lighting note and pause/fullscreen controls. No customization sidebar.
FORM: user-pinned illustrated room with pixel characters, selected directly in chat; code-led layout. Morning and night art keep the same composition. Signature behavior: a device-clock-driven blend and subtle blinking; reduced motion uses static sprites.
FINISH: inspect desktop/mobile and both lighting states, test time boundaries and approved-asset integrity, record the shipped design and asset prompts.
