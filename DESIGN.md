## Overview
Inherit the supplied Nestle visual system. Let the real 3D assets lead, with customization alongside a warm miniature room.

## Colors
Independent pink and dark palettes; preserve natural character colors across themes. Semantic CSS variables are the implementation source.

## Typography
Nunito, self-hosted where available. Rounded headings and legible controls.

## Layout
Desktop: interactive room beside a narrow customization panel. Mobile: room above controls. No fictional app tabs.

## Elevation & Depth
Soft 3D shadows ground characters. Controls use borders rather than decorative shadow stacks.

## Shapes
Rounded controls inherit the reference. Pills are for compact segmented choices.

## Components
Character switcher, preset selects, labeled color swatches, animation buttons, camera and theme controls. Visible focus, loading, error, and reduced-motion support.

## Do's and Don'ts
Show real available animations. Identify the supplied pack and missing assets. Do not label placeholders as 3D models.
